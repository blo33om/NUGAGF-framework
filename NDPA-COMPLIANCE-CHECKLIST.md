# NDPA 2023 Compliance Checklist

To be completed by the Data Protection Officer (or designated lead) before deploying any tool, process, or vendor under the Generative AI Use Policy. Reference: Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023; NDPC General Application and Implementation Directive (GAID) 2025.

## Section A — Lawful Basis

- [ ] A lawful basis for processing student submissions has been identified and documented (e.g., legitimate academic interest, performance of institutional function).
- [ ] The lawful basis has been communicated to students via the published Generative AI Use Policy.

## Section B — Third-Party Tools and Vendors

- [ ] Any external AI-detection or plagiarism-checking tool has been identified and listed.
- [ ] A Data Processing Agreement (DPA) is in place with each vendor, per GAID 2025 requirements.
- [ ] The vendor's data storage location and retention period have been confirmed and documented.

## Section C — Student Notification and Rights

- [ ] Students have been clearly informed of how their submitted work may be reviewed, processed, or stored.
- [ ] A process exists for students to exercise data subject rights (e.g., access, correction) in relation to submissions processed under this framework.

## Section D — High-Risk Processing

- [ ] Any high-risk processing activity (e.g., biometric verification, large-scale automated profiling) has been identified.
- [ ] A Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) has been completed for any such activity before deployment.
- [ ] The NDPC has been consulted where residual risk remains high after mitigation, per NDPA guidance.

## Section E — Governance and Review

- [ ] The AI Governance Committee has approved the use of the tool/process.
- [ ] A review date has been set (recommended: annually, or upon vendor/tool change).
- [ ] This checklist is filed and retained as part of the institution's compliance records.
