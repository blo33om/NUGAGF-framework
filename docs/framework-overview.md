# NUGAGF Framework Overview

## Background

Generative AI (GenAI) tools have become widely used by students in Nigerian public universities, often without institutional oversight. Peer-reviewed evidence shows that:

1. AI-text detection tools are not reliable enough to serve as a stand-alone enforcement mechanism (Weber-Wulff et al., 2023; Elkhatat et al., 2023).
2. International governance frameworks such as UNESCO's Guidance for Generative AI in Education and Research and the U.S. NIST AI Risk Management Framework offer sound general principles but are not adapted to the Nigerian regulatory and cultural context.
3. Nigerian studies confirm a high prevalence of GenAI-facilitated academic dishonesty, but none had translated these findings into an implementable governance artefact prior to this framework.

## The Four Pillars

### 1. Govern
Establish clear institutional ownership of GenAI governance through a dedicated, cross-functional AI Governance Committee (see `COMMITTEE-TOR.md`) and a formally adopted policy (see `POLICY-TEMPLATE.md`).

### 2. Map
Conduct a systematic, faculty-by-faculty audit of assessment types to classify their exposure to GenAI misuse (see `ASSESSMENT-RISK-MAPPING.md`), and document where and how student data is processed in the course of governance activities.

### 3. Measure
Adopt a layered evidence model for investigating suspected misuse, in which AI-detection software output is used only as a weak, corroborating signal rather than definitive proof — a direct response to documented detector unreliability.

### 4. Manage
Apply graduated, proportionate responses to suspected misuse (education first, formal process for repeat/serious cases), supported by continuous staff and student training and an annual policy review cycle.

## Legal Alignment

All data-processing activity connected to this framework is designed to comply with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023 and the NDPC's General Application and Implementation Directive (GAID) 2025 — see `NDPA-COMPLIANCE-CHECKLIST.md`.

## Source Basis

This framework structure adapts the NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0, 2023) Govern–Map–Measure–Manage structure, informed by UNESCO's 2023 Guidance for Generative AI in Education and Research, and grounded in Nigerian empirical literature on GenAI misuse in higher education. Full citations are available in the accompanying seminar paper.

## Suggested Adoption Path

1. Present this framework to the Academic Affairs / Quality Assurance unit.
2. Establish the AI Governance Committee using `COMMITTEE-TOR.md`.
3. Adapt `POLICY-TEMPLATE.md` with institution-specific details and route for formal approval.
4. Roll out `ASSESSMENT-RISK-MAPPING.md` to faculties for the upcoming academic session.
5. Have the Data Protection Officer complete `NDPA-COMPLIANCE-CHECKLIST.md` before any detection tool or vendor is engaged.
