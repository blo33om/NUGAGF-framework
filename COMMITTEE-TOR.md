# Terms of Reference: AI Governance Committee

**Version:** 1.1
**Owning Body:** [Senate / Academic Board]

## 1. Purpose

The AI Governance Committee ("the Committee") is established to own, implement, and review the institution's Generative AI Use Policy (see `POLICY-TEMPLATE.md`), in line with the Nigerian University Generative AI Governance Framework (NUGAGF).

## 2. Membership

- Chair: Deputy Vice-Chancellor (Academic) or delegated nominee
- Representative from the Registry / Academic Affairs
- Head, ICT/IT Unit
- Data Protection Officer
- Two Faculty Deans (rotating annually)
- One Student Union representative
- One representative from the Directorate of Academic Planning/Quality Assurance
- Departmental Academic Integrity Officers (attend as required, non-voting, for case reporting)

Members serve a two-year renewable term, except the Student Union representative, whose term follows the Union's own election cycle. A quorum for decision-making is five members, including the Chair or their delegate and the Data Protection Officer.

## 3. Responsibilities

- Approve and periodically review the institution's Generative AI Use Policy.
- Hear escalated academic integrity cases involving disputed or ambiguous GenAI use, and appeals referred under Section 9 of the Policy.
- Approve any third-party tool or vendor involved in reviewing or processing student submissions, in coordination with the Data Protection Officer.
- Commission annual staff and student training on GenAI use.
- Monitor emerging GenAI-related risks and recommend policy updates.
- Review semester case reports submitted by Departmental Academic Integrity Officers and identify institution-wide patterns (e.g., a faculty with disproportionately high Level 2/3 cases, indicating a need for assessment redesign).
- Report annually to Senate/Academic Board on policy implementation and case statistics.

## 4. What the Committee Does Not Do

To avoid role confusion, the Committee does not conduct first-line investigations of individual cases (this sits with Departmental Academic Integrity Officers under Section 8.3 of the Policy) and does not itself impose Level 3 sanctions (these follow the institution's existing Student Disciplinary Regulations). The Committee's role is oversight, policy ownership, escalation, and pattern review, not day-to-day case handling.

## 5. Meeting Frequency

The Committee shall meet at least once per semester, and additionally as required to address escalated cases or appeals. Emergency meetings may be convened by the Chair with 72 hours' notice where a matter requires urgent attention.

## 6. Reporting Line

The Committee reports to [Senate / Academic Board / relevant governing body], through the Office of the Deputy Vice-Chancellor (Academic).

## 7. Documentation and Records

The Committee shall maintain a confidential case log of all Level 2 and Level 3 outcomes reported to it, for the purposes of pattern monitoring and annual reporting, in compliance with the institution's Data Protection Policy and the NDPA 2023. Individual case records shall not be shared outside the Committee except as required for a disciplinary hearing or appeal.

## 8. Review of these Terms of Reference

These Terms of Reference shall be reviewed alongside the Generative AI Use Policy on an annual basis.
